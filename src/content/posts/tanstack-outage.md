## Introduction
On May 17, 2026, TanStack experienced a major outage that affected users worldwide. This article examines the implications of this downtime and how developers can respond effectively.

## What Happened?
The outage began early in the morning and lasted for several hours, impacting various TanStack libraries and their functionalities. Users reported issues accessing core features, which hindered development processes.

## Consequences of the Outage
With TanStack being a critical tool for React developers, the outage had several consequences:
- **Development Delays:** Many teams faced setbacks as they could not rely on the functionality they were accustomed to.
- **User Frustration:** End-users experienced disruptions, leading to concerns about the reliability of applications built using TanStack.

### Key Insights
Following the incident, many developers shared insights within the community. It's essential to:
- **Communicate Effectively**: Keep your team informed about these outages to set realistic expectations.
- **Plan for Contingencies**: Always have a backup plan when relying on third-party libraries.

## Recovery Strategies
Post-outage, here are some strategies developers should consider:
1. **Monitoring**: Implement monitoring tools to quickly identify issues in the future.
2. **Documentation**: Keep detailed documentation of your app’s dependencies to make recovery faster.
3. **Community Engagement**: Engage with the TanStack community for support and the latest updates on outages.

## Conclusion
The recent TanStack outage highlighted the importance of reliability in software dependencies. By being prepared and informed, developers can mitigate the risks associated with such incidents in the future.
